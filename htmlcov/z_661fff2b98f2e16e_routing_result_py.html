275class ShortestPathResult: 

276 """Distances and paths from the same Dijkstra searches. 

277 

278 ``distances`` is a sparse pandas Series or DataFrame, a NumPy array, or a 

279 SciPy CSR array, depending on ``distance_format``. Array positions match 

280 the pandas path table, with one distance per row for one-dimensional 

281 arrays. Accessing ``paths`` creates and caches the path table. With 

282 ``path_storage="lazy"``, use ``get_path`` to reconstruct individual routes without materializing that
