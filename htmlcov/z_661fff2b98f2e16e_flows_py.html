1"""Flow assignment along shortest-path forests: trips from sources spread over destinations by attraction and cost. 

2 

3Every cluster of sources (for example the entrances of one block) sends its summed flow to the nodes it reaches 

4within ``cutoff``. A reached node takes a share proportional to its attraction times a decay of the travel cost; 

5the shares travel back along the shortest-path tree, so every edge carries the flow of all the trips that pass 

6it. The result is an all-or-nothing assignment per search: equilibrium methods (BPR, MSA) repeat it with edge 

7costs updated between runs. 

8"""

38 """Assign flows from sources to attractive nodes along shortest paths and sum them on the edges. 

39 

40 Sources are grouped into clusters by ``cluster_offsets`` (cluster ``c`` holds the sources 

41 ``cluster_offsets[c]:cluster_offsets[c + 1]``; by default every source is a cluster of its own). Each cluster 

42 runs one search from all of its sources at once, every node joining its nearest source (equal costs go to the 

43 source that comes first in ``nodes_gdf``). Every reached node other than a source is a destination with the weight 

44 ``node_weights[node] * decay(cost)``: 

45 

46 - ``"gaussian"``: ``exp(-0.5 * ((cost - mean) / std) ** 2)``, trips of a typical length ``mean``; 

47 - ``"exp"``: ``exp(-beta * cost)``; 

48 - ``"flat"``: 1. 

49 

50 The summed flow of the cluster's sources is split between its destinations in proportion to these weights and 

51 carried back along the search tree. An arc keeps the flow of one cluster only when it exceeds ``min_flow`` 

52 or when the arc leaves a source and the flow is positive; the kept flows of all clusters are summed. 

53 

54 Args: 

55 urban_graph: Graph to load. 

56 source_nodes: Graph node ids of the sources, grouped by cluster. 

57 source_flows: Flow sent from every source (trips, vehicles), finite and non-negative. 

58 node_weights: Attraction of every node: a ``nodes_gdf`` column name, a Series indexed by node id or an array 

59 in ``nodes_gdf`` order. Missing values count as 0. 

60 cluster_offsets: ``n_clusters + 1`` ascending positions into ``source_nodes`` starting at 0 and ending at 

61 ``len(source_nodes)``; ``None`` makes every source a cluster. 

62 decay: Decay of the destination weight with the travel cost. 

63 mean: Typical trip cost for ``"gaussian"``, in the units of ``weight``. 

64 std: Spread of trip costs for ``"gaussian"``, positive. 

65 beta: Rate of ``"exp"``, per unit of ``weight``. 

66 cutoff: Largest travel cost of a trip, inclusive; ``None`` for no limit. 

67 min_flow: Flow of one cluster below which an arc that does not leave a source is ignored. 

68 weight: Edge cost column: ``time_min`` (minutes, the default) or ``length_meter`` (metres). 

69 per: ``"edges"`` (default) for a table of the edges; ``"arcs"`` for one value per arc of 

70 ``urban_graph.adjacency_matrix`` (aligned with its ``data``, after the call), where parallel edges are 

71 one arc. 

72 max_workers: Number of threads; ``None`` uses all cores. 

73 

74 Returns: 

75 With ``per="arcs"``, a float64 array of the arc flows. Otherwise a DataFrame indexed like ``edges_gdf`` 

76 with ``flow_uv`` (along the edge from ``u`` to ``v``), ``flow_vu`` (against it, for edges travelled both 

77 ways) and their sum ``flow``. Among parallel edges only the cheapest one carries flow; equally cheap ones 

78 share it. 

79 """
