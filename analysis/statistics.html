Network statistics¶

Overview¶

The statistics functions describe a graph that is already built: its size, density, junctions, shape and connectivity, for the whole graph, inside a study area, for every zone of a grid or around every node.

Function

Result

graph_stats()

Measures of the whole network, of the part inside area or of every one of zones

local_stats()

The same kind of measures around every node, within a network radius

edge_stats()

Counts, lengths and speeds for every value of an edge column (type, highway, category)

transport_stats()

Stops, routes, stop spacing and speed of every public-transport mode

streets_per_node()

The number of streets that meet at every node

route_directness()

Network distance over straight distance between origins and destinations

 
stats = iduedu.graph_stats(walk, area=district)              # one Series of measures
by_square = iduedu.graph_stats(walk, zones=grid)             # one row per zone
around = iduedu.local_stats(walk, [400, 800])                # one row per node
iduedu.edge_stats(intermodal, by="type")                     # one row per edge type

Inputs¶

Every function reads an UrbanGraph in a projected CRS with lengths in metres. area follows the rule of territory in the graph builders: a bare shapely geometry is in EPSG:4326 (longitude, latitude), and a GeoSeries or GeoDataFrame carries its own CRS. zones is a GeoSeries or GeoDataFrame in any CRS. Both are reprojected to the graph CRS. edge_types restricts the measures to the edges of the given type, for example ["walk"] on an intermodal graph.

local_stats() measures its radius in metres by length_meter (the default) or in minutes with weight="time_min". Several radii are computed from the same searches.

Method¶

Streets¶

An OSM graph stores a two-way street as two directed edges, and a walk graph can store the same segment twice. The statistics count a street: one undirected connection between two nodes, however many directed or parallel edges the graph stores for it. Lengths are taken on the unique segments of the geometry (unique_segments()), so the two directions of a street add their length once. A node with one street is a dead end, and a node with three or more streets is an intersection.

The number of streets at a node always comes from the whole graph. With area or zones the nodes belong to the zone they lie in, and streets and segments to the zone of their midpoint. A graph built with a margin around the study area therefore gives the true number of streets at the nodes near the border. Nodes with one street closer than boundary_buffer (1 m) to the boundary of area are streets cut by clipping and are not counted as dead ends.

Measures of the network¶

graph_stats() returns the following groups of measures; a measure that is undefined for a graph or a zone is NaN.

Group

Measures

Size

node_count, edge_count (edges as stored), street_count, street_length_total (metres), street_length_mean

Junctions

streets_per_node_mean, dead_end_count, dead_end_share, intersection_count, intersection_4way_share (four or more streets, among the intersections)

Shape

circuity_mean (street length over the straight distance between its ends), self_loop_share, parallel_street_share, orientation_entropy and orientation_order

Connectivity

component_count, largest_component_share, beta_index, gamma_index, cyclomatic_number, meshedness

Density

area_km2, node_density, intersection_density (per km²) and street_density (km per km²), with area or zones

Edge attributes

oneway_share (of street length), speed_mean_kmh, elevation and grade after add_elevation(), platform and boarding counts on graphs with public transport

The connectivity indices compare the number of streets e with the number of nodes v and of components p: the beta index is e / v, the gamma index e / (3 (v - 2)), the cyclomatic number e - v + p (independent cycles) and the meshedness (e - v + p) / (2 v - 5). The denominators are the largest numbers of streets and cycles a planar graph can have. Only streets with both ends inside the area are counted for them.

The orientation measures sort the bearings of the segments into 36 bins of 10°, each segment weighted by its length. orientation_entropy is the Shannon entropy of the bins in nats, and orientation_order rescales it to 1 for a grid of two perpendicular directions and 0 for bearings spread evenly over all bins (Boeing, 2019).

Measures around every node¶

local_stats() runs one cut-off search from every node and collects the nodes within radius. Every street gives half of its length to each of its two ends, so the street length around a node is the sum of the halves held by the nodes in reach. For every radius r the result has the columns node_count_r, intersection_count_r, dead_end_count_r, street_length_r (metres), streets_per_node_mean_r, dead_end_share_r and intersections_per_km_r (intersections per kilometre of street). The node itself is included.

Directness¶

route_directness() divides the shortest network distance by the straight distance between the same two nodes. A value of 1 is a straight route; the mean over a street grid is about 1.27. Without origins it takes sample random nodes (200 by default) as both origins and destinations. The result is a table with origins in rows and destinations in columns, and result.stack().mean() is the mean directness.

Outputs¶

On the walk graph of Vasilyevsky Island (22 289 nodes, 726 km of streets), graph_stats(walk) reports 14 244 intersections, 22 % of the nodes as dead ends, a mean circuity of 1.04 and an orientation order of 0.27. A random sample of 200 nodes gives a mean directness of 1.25. On 16 threads the whole graph is measured in 0.06 s, 85 squares of 500 m in 0.14 s and every node within 400 and 800 m in 0.17 s.

Functions¶

graph_stats

Measures of size, density, shape and connectivity of a street network.

local_stats

Network measures around every node, within a travel cost along the network.

edge_stats

Edge counts, lengths and speeds for every value of an edge column.

transport_stats

Stops, routes and stop spacing of every public-transport mode of a graph.

streets_per_node

Number of streets that meet at every node.

route_directness

Ratio of the shortest network distance to the straight distance between origins and destinations.

Figures¶

Limitations¶

  • Junctions drawn as several nodes, such as dual carriageways and the crossings of a walk graph, inflate node and intersection counts. Merge them with consolidate_intersections() before comparing graphs from different sources.

  • Densities depend on the zone boundaries; small zones and zones cut by water give unstable values.

  • local_stats() near the border of a graph counts only the network inside it. Build the graph with a margin at least as wide as the largest radius.

  • route_directness() represents objects by their nearest nodes, so the straight distance is measured between nodes, not between the objects.

References¶
